# Can a third view recover the surfaces the second camera cannot see?

Freeze the analytic 3×3 patch matcher from the parallax benchmark. On new quartets of shapes, compare its depth predictions with one translated context image and with two opposite-side translated images. For each candidate depth use the smaller of its two patch errors. Score reference pixels hidden in the first translated view that become visible in the second. This subset uses renderer ray IDs for evaluation alone. Report new correct pixels and regressions, all-pixel accuracy, the still-hidden group, and intervals clustering all four worlds in each layout. Duplicate the first view with its original baseline as a strict no-extra-information control; separately use the other view from a different depth assignment of the identical reference layout, at the correct second-camera baseline, as a mismatched-view control. Ground-truth depth does not enter any prediction. Use 512 independent layouts at the previous held-out shift, seed 492731. Confirm at a new seed (743915) and one-unit instead of two-unit first/second shifts only if the matched third camera improves by at least ten points on newly visible pixels, the paired interval excludes zero, matched beats mismatched there, and aggregate accuracy drops by under two points. Otherwise stop. No retraining or width sweep.
