# Screen the DTU robot path for matched stereo directions

After the ETH3D scene screen found too few vertical moves, qualify the predetermined robot path of the [official DTU SampleSet](https://roboimagedata.compute.dtu.dk/?page_id=36). The path repeats across scenes. The dataset includes structured-light geometry in the frame of the calibration matrices. Use the first 49 positions, which are acquired in both DTU camera sets. Fetch only the camera projection text entries through HTTP Range requests. Attribute this citeware data to [Jensen et al., CVPR 2014](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2014/html/Jensen_Large_Scale_Multi-view_2014_CVPR_paper.html).

Require 40 mm transverse translation and an optical-axis translation no greater than 45% of total translation. Require direction within 30 degrees of horizontal or vertical, viewing-direction change up to 35 degrees, and a matched camera pair with transverse baseline ratio at most 1.25 and a viewing-angle difference at most ten degrees. Choose from projection geometry before inspecting photometric quality. The screening threshold is 12 eligible reference poses. DTU does not supply ETH3D sparse COLMAP tracks. This step qualifies pose balance only. A later pilot must check common visibility against independent scene geometry before crediting an accuracy result.
