# Qualify real camera triplets before running an active stereo benchmark

Use four scenes from the [official ETH3D high-resolution training dataset](https://www.eth3d.net/datasets#high-res-multi-view). Courtyard, delivery area, facade and pipes have pre-undistorted images with calibration and independently measured laser depths. Aim to find reference cameras with one predominantly horizontal and one predominantly vertical candidate displacement at equivalent strength. The calibration-only stage sees camera centers, rotations and shared COLMAP track IDs. It does not inspect image intensities, laser depths, or image-matching outcomes. [Calibration and depth format](https://www.eth3d.net/documentation) · [Schöps et al., CVPR 2017](https://www.eth3d.net/data/schoeps2017cvpr.pdf)

Express translation in the reference camera frame. Require the transverse displacement to be at least 4 cm, axial component at most 45% of total translation and viewing-direction change at most 35 degrees. A baseline is horizontal or vertical when its angle to that unoriented axis is at most 30 degrees. Each candidate must share at least 100 sparse tracks and at least 15% of the reference's tracked points. Pair candidates with transverse length ratio at most 1.25, rotation-angle difference at most 10 degrees, and shared-track overlap fractions within 0.15. Choose the pair minimizing the sum of scaled mismatch terms, using image IDs to break numerical ties. The screen qualifies if it yields at least 24 reference triplets across at least three scenes, with at least four triplets in each counted scene. Publish camera IDs and metrics and note that cameras in the same scene are correlated.

If geometry qualifies, retain the selected views for a paired natural-scene study. Compare reference-only patch repetition with a structure-tensor cue, both fixed orientations and random assignment. Enforce one secondary camera and an identical calibrated depth estimator for every policy. Depth accuracy uses independent laser data and comes only after the geometry screen. Depth maps are supplied in the corresponding distorted image coordinates and must be mapped correctly to the undistorted cameras. No dataset images or laser archive files are distributed with this repository.
